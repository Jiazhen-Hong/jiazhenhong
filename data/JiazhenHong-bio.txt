Jiazhen Hong is an AI Research Scientist at EMOTIV and holds a Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from Rutgers University. He develops foundation models for neural signals and builds real-time brain-computer interfaces that use large language models for assistive communication. His research also spans multimodal learning, human-machine interfaces, and AI for healthcare and cognitive aging. His work has appeared in Scientific Reports, IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, and IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering, among other venues.

At EMOTIV, he develops large-scale EEG foundation models using distributed GPU training. He has represented EMOTIV in research discussions with Nike Research and Meta Brain, demonstrated EEG-X and its NeuralBench results at Meta’s Open Source AI for Science Summit, and hosted EMOTIV’s live EEG demo and Q&A at NeuroNight SF.

He holds an M.Sc. from Stevens Institute of Technology, B.Sc. double degrees from Jimei University and Chung Yuan Christian University, and previously conducted machine learning and biostatistics research at Harvard Medical School.
