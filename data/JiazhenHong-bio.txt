Jiazhen Hong is an AI Research Scientist at EMOTIV and holds a Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from Rutgers University. His research focuses on foundation models, large language models, multimodal learning, and scalable AI systems for biomedical and time-series data, with applications in healthcare AI, brain-computer interfaces, and assistive communication. He develops neural foundation models and locally deployed LLM-integrated P300 speller systems. His work has appeared in Scientific Reports, IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering, NeurIPS, ICDM, EMBC, NER, and ISBI.

At EMOTIV, Jiazhen develops large-scale neural foundation models, long-context architectures, distributed multi-GPU training pipelines, and self-supervised representations that transfer across datasets and downstream tasks. His related work includes first-author publications at NeurIPS and IEEE ICDM workshops.

He holds an M.Sc. from Stevens Institute of Technology, B.Sc. double degrees from Jimei University and Chung Yuan Christian University, and previously conducted machine learning and biostatistics research at Harvard Medical School.
