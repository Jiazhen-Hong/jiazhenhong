02 / HOW IT WORKS

Three ideas,
one robust model.

Follow one EEG recording through the pipeline. Choose a stage to see what is learned and why it matters.

DEVICE VARIABILITY

Position, not device identity

Each channel receives an embedding from its scalp coordinates. Nearby electrodes get similar representations, even when headset names and layouts differ.

WHY IT HELPS

A pretrained model can understand a new 8-, 14-, or 22-channel layout without relearning every channel from scratch.

A CLOSER LOOK / DiCT

Same shape.
Different timing.

Direct MSE can punish a phase shift more than a missing frequency. DiCT compares signals after multi-scale convolution, making the loss more aware of structure.
